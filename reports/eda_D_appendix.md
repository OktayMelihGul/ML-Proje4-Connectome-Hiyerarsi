## Items in full

- **D-1 Degrees (τ = 10).** Median in/out-degree: fafb all 3/3, central 4/5, optic 3/3; malecns all 6/8, central 11/14, optic 5/7.
  Central neurons are 4.4–5.4× as likely as optic neurons to have ≥ 100 input partners (fafb 1.54% vs 0.28%; malecns 4.40% vs 1.00%),
  so the optic lobes pull the all-neuron curve towards low degrees. The single largest degrees are optic (out-degree up to 4,704 fafb, 6,727 malecns).
  → The population choice (D14) changes every degree statistic; malecns degrees are about twice fafb's at the same τ.
- **D-2 Hubs (top 1% of central by in+out at τ = 10).** fafb 445 hubs (total degree ≥ 208), malecns 440 (≥ 335); 90% are super_class central,
  ~9%/7% descending. They are mostly input hubs (in > out for 58% fafb, 66% malecns; median out/in 0.89 and 0.75). Families: AVLP/PVLP
  visual-auditory neurons, antennal-lobe local neurons (lLN), MBONs, AOTU; top regions AVLP and AL.
  → Hubs are central integrators, not a few giant optic cells; they shorten paths into AVLP/AL, which Project 4 must keep in mind for depth.
- **D-3 Zero in / zero out (central).** At τ = 10: no input fafb 20.7% / malecns 7.2%, no output 15.8% / 3.1%, both 11.0% / 1.9%.
  At τ = 31: 62.8% / 35.7% no input. At τ = 1 it is ≤ 1.8%. Relative 2.6% leaves 37–43% with no output.
  → The zero-vs-null choice (D16) touches about one central neuron in five in fafb at the default threshold, and four times fewer in malecns.
- **D-4 Seed sets.** Sensory seeds: fafb 17,516 (visual 11,391, mechanosensory 2,667, olfactory 2,281, AN-class sensory_ascending 526,
  gustatory 410); malecns brain 9,142 (visual 4,107, olfactory 2,639, mechanosensory 1,673, gustatory 351, proprioceptive 131). Motor seeds:
  110 vs 107, almost all in GNG (89 / 97).
  → The seed sets differ by modality, mostly visual (photoreceptors: 11.4k vs 4.1k); every depth is counted from different starting sets.
- **D-5 Reachability (BFS from all sensory, whole graph).** Central non-sensory neurons reachable: fafb 99.9 / 96.5 / 87.7 / 40.6% at τ 1 / 5 / 10 / 31
  (relative 0.9%: 99.8, 2.6%: 93.7); malecns 99.9 / 99.7 / 98.2 / 70.3% (0.9%: 99.7, 2.6%: 83.0). Median depth 2 hops at τ = 1, 3 (fafb) and 2 (malecns) at τ = 10; max depth 7 / 8 at τ = 10, up to 13–14 at τ = 31 or relative 2.6%.
  → Reachability depends strongly on weak edges: going from τ = 5 to τ = 10 makes 3,310 more fafb neurons unreachable; at τ = 31 most of fafb is.
- **D-6 Who is unreachable (τ = 10, central non-sensory).** fafb 4,615 unreachable: 24.1% ascending (vs 1.9% of reachable), 65.0% central (vs 89.2%),
  31.2% motor_periphery regions (vs 16.1%); median total degree 2 vs 14, 33% with degree 0. malecns 713 unreachable: 20.9% ascending (vs 2.4%),
  19.8% visual_projection (vs 11.8%), median degree 4 vs 30.
  → The two sets differ in class, region and degree, so dropping unreachable neurons biases any depth statistic (Project 4's central claim, descriptively confirmed).
- **D-7 Reciprocity (τ = 10) vs degree-preserving null (20 seeds).** Whole graph: fafb 0.115 vs null 0.0026 (44×), malecns 0.127 vs 0.0021 (60×).
  All 20 top regions in both datasets lie above the null 97.5% bound. Highest: EB 0.54/0.52, MB_ML_L 0.52, AL 0.41–0.47, FB 0.20/0.28; lowest LOP 0.05–0.07.
  Randomiser: every run reached its swap target, no mixing warning; 5.8 s (fafb, 1.07M edges) and 14.0 s (malecns, 2.14M edges) per seed, ~6.6 min in total (wall-clock; varies a little between runs).
  → Reciprocity is far above what degrees explain everywhere, strongest in recurrent circuits (central complex, mushroom body, antennal lobe).
- **D-7b Reciprocity vs a region-preserving null (added after the session; `src/region_null.py`, 20 seeds).** The D-7 null rewires across the whole brain:
  62% of real τ = 10 edges join two neurons of the same region, but only 7% do in that null (fafb, seed 0), so its per-region values come from small, different edge sets.
  Here swaps pair only edges of the same (pre region, post region) block, so each neuron keeps its exact degree to and from every region.
  Whole graph: fafb 0.115 vs 0.031 (3.7×; D-7 null 44×), malecns 0.127 vs 0.029 (4.4×; D-7 null 60×). Above the new null's 97.5% bound in 18 of 19 informative
  fafb regions (LOP_L 0.052 vs 0.053, inside the band) and 20/20 malecns. Ratios: ME 83–101×, GNG 8.2/8.9×, FB 8.0/8.3×, EB 8.1/3.8×, SMP/SLP/LH/PVLP 3.8–6.6×,
  AVLP 2.8–3.2×, AL 1.9–2.3×, LOP 1.0–3.8×. fafb MB_ML_L: null not informative. The block is dominated by APL and DPM (76% of its edges, 98% of its reciprocal edges),
  so few swaps are possible: half the swap target, 21% of its edges changed; null 0.503 vs observed 0.524. Mixing elsewhere: every other top-20 block reached its
  target with 68–99% of edges changed; edges in blocks below target 1.6% (fafb) / 1.4% (malecns), 790 / 1,214 of them in blocks with no alternative arrangement;
  16.2 s / 39.0 s per seed, ~18 min in total (wall-clock). Limitation: region = each neuron's primary neuropil; blocking edges by the neuropil of their synapses (edgeroi) is not done.
  → Region structure alone gives about a quarter of the observed reciprocity (0.031 of 0.115; 0.029 of 0.127), so the D-7 factor of 44–60× overstates the excess
  (~4× against this null), but reciprocity stays above chance in almost every region;
  the excess is largest in the medulla, central complex and GNG and smallest in the antennal lobe and lobula plate.

**Surprise:** in the brain-only malecns, 14.2% of ascending neurons are unreachable at τ = 10 (49.1% at τ = 31), while on the full CNS
(week 2 setting) only 1.3% are: cutting the nerve cord removes the inputs that reach ascending neurons. fafb, which never had a nerve cord, is far worse
(63.5% at τ = 10). Unreachability is therefore partly an artefact of the imaged volume, not only of the threshold.
